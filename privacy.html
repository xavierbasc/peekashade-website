Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Peekashade does not collect any personal data. None. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

What the app stores

The app saves your settings (music and sound volume) and your progress on your own device, in the app's private storage. This never leaves the device and is deleted with the app.

Network

The app contains no networking code: it works entirely offline.

Children

The app is designed for young children and follows Apple's Kids Category rules: no behavioural advertising, no data collection, and links out of the app (settings, quitting) are behind a control that has to be held down.
