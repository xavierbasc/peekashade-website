Questions

Before you buy

What age is it for?

Two to four. Toddlers who can drag a finger will manage on their own; younger ones enjoy the sounds with a grown-up.

Is there any reading?

None. Everything is shapes, shadows and sound, so it works in any language — and before any of them.

How long is a session?

As long as you like. Each show is a couple of minutes and rolls straight into the next, so it is easy to stop after one.
