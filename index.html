Peekashade

Who is hiding?

A little shadow theatre for toddlers: drag each friend onto its shadow and the show goes on.

Where to play What is it?

Ages 2–4 · iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows, Linux and Android · $2.99, one payment

How it works

Pick a show at the ticket booth, drag the pieces from the velvet tray onto the shadows on the paper screen, and every completed show ends with a BRAVO and a curtain call.

What's inside

Twelve little shows

Farm, jungle, ocean, road, sky, music, bugs, food, dinosaurs, home, weather — and a MIX that shuffles them all.

Made for small hands

Big pieces, no rotation and a generous snap: if it lands near its shadow, it fits.

Nobody ever loses

No timers, no score, no game over. A wrong drop just wobbles and comes back.

88 friends to meet

Every character has its own sound, drawn and synthesised from scratch.

For grown-ups

Peekashade is made to be handed to a small child without worrying:

  • No ads and no in-app purchases. One payment, that's it.
  • No data collection. The game has no accounts, no analytics and no network code at all.
  • No links out. Settings and the quit button sit behind a button you must hold for two seconds.
  • Works offline, on the plane and in the car.
  • Gentle sound: music sits low under the effects, and both have their own volume.
