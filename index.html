Questions

What age is it for?

Two to four. Toddlers who can drag a finger will manage on their own; younger ones enjoy the sounds with a grown-up.

Is there any reading?

None. Everything is shapes, shadows and sound.

How long is a session?

As long as you like. Each show is a couple of minutes and rolls straight into the next.

Ready for the first show?

One payment. Works on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
