Support

Something not working, or an idea for Peekashade? Write to javier.bascones@gmail.com — you'll get an answer from the person who made it, usually within a couple of days.

Common questions

How do I open the settings?

On the title screen, hold the gear button for two seconds. Inside the game, hold the house button for one second to go back.

The music is too loud.

Music and sound effects have separate sliders in the grown-ups panel, and the music already sits low under the effects.

Does it need internet?

No. The game has no networking code at all.

I bought it on iPhone. Do I pay again on Mac?

No. It is a universal purchase: buy once and it works on iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account.

My child tapped something and left the game.

That should not happen: everything that leaves the game is behind a button you have to hold down. If you find a way out, please tell me.

Requirements

iOS 15 or later, or macOS 11 or later. The game runs entirely offline.
