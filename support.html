Frequently asked

How do I open the settings? On the title screen, hold the gear button for two seconds. Inside a game, hold the house button for one second to go back.

The music is too loud (or too quiet). Music and sound effects have separate sliders in the grown-ups panel.

Does it need internet? No, never.

One payment? Yes: $2.99, no ads and no in-app purchases. Buy it once and it works on iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account.

Requirements

iOS 15 or later, or macOS 11 or later.
